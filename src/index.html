How to play:
1) You start. Choose one of the bottom 6 pits to begin.
2) The AI goes after you.
3) If your last stone ends in your own store then you get to go again.
4) If your last stone ends in one of your pits that is empty, your opponent's stones in the pit directly accross are taken by you and placed in your store.
5) When either you or the AI has no stones left in any of their 6 pits, the opponent places all of their remianing stones in their store. The game is over.
6) The player with the most stones in their store at the end of the game wins.

Choose a difficulty mode and begin!
